# Privacy Policy for Poop-Tracker

**Effective Date:** October 26, 2025

## 1. Introduction
Welcome to **Poop-Tracker**. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal data. This privacy policy will inform you as to how we look after your personal data when you visit our application and tell you about your privacy rights and how the law protects you.

## 2. Data We Collect
We collect the following types of data:
- **Health Data**: Logs of your bowel movements (Bristol scale, time, etc.) which are stored locally on your device or in your private cloud database.
- **Usage Data**: Information about how you use our app.

## 3. How We Use Your Data
We use your data to:
- Provide and manage your account.
- Visualize your health statistics.
- Improve our application.

## 4. Data Storage
- **Local Storage**: Most data is stored locally on your device.
- **Cloud Storage**: If you enable cloud sync, data is encrypted and stored securely on Supabase servers.

## 5. Third-Party Services
We may use third-party services such as:
- **Supabase**: For authentication and database hosting.
- **Google Gemini**: For AI-powered insights (only if you opt-in).
