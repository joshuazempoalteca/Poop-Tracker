## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: How do I delete my account?**
A: Go to Profile > Delete Account. This will permanently remove your data.

**Q: I lost my guest mode data.**
A: Guest mode data is stored locally on your device. If you delete the app, the data is lost.

**Q: Is my data private?**
A: Yes. In guest mode, data never leaves your device. When logged in, data is stored securely in our private database.
